## 01. The Story Behind Every Thread

Some things are made to occupy a space. Others are made to give it a soul.

**Khan Carpets International (KCI)** is a heritage-led Indian carpet house rooted in the celebrated weaving traditions of Bhadohi, Uttar Pradesh — a region whose identity has been shaped, for generations, by the quiet artistry of its looms.

Established in 1974, the house carries forward a legacy of hand-knotted craftsmanship, bringing together traditional weaving knowledge, considered design and an understanding of contemporary interiors.

Across three generations, the craft has remained at the heart of the enterprise. What begins with a thread, a loom and a skilled hand travels far beyond its place of origin — finding its way into homes, hospitality spaces and design houses across more than thirty countries.

At Khan Carpets International, a carpet is never merely a floor covering. It is an expression of patience, material, memory and the enduring beauty of things made by hand.

## 02. Our Philosophy

**Every knot carries a country across the world.**

We believe that true craftsmanship cannot be hurried, and that the most meaningful objects are those in which human skill remains visible.

Our philosophy rests upon three enduring principles:

- **Heritage:** Preserving the generational knowledge and weaving traditions of India's carpet-making communities.
- **Individuality:** Creating carpets that respond to distinct spaces, palettes, dimensions and design sensibilities.
- **Integrity:** Recognising the people behind the process and respecting the hands that bring every design to life.

Our work belongs equally to the past and the present — honouring the traditions that shaped it while remaining open to the possibilities of modern design.

## 03. The Craft of Making

A rug is roughly one million decisions, taken by hand.

From the selection of fibres to the final finishing, each stage contributes to the character of the finished piece.

Our craftsmanship encompasses:

| Element | Details |
|---|---|
| Materials | Wool, silk, bamboo silk and jute |
| Knot density | 100–400 KPSI |
| Production lead time | 10–26 weeks |
| Bespoke orders | Starting from a single piece |
| Finishing | Washing, hand-shearing and detailed finishing |
| Design | Custom dimensions, colourways and CAD development |

Small-lot dyeing, careful washing and hand-finishing remain integral to the process.

We also state our commitment to no child labour and fair wages, with a named-weaver certificate accompanying every carpet shipped.

## 04. The Collections

Four distinct expressions of Indian carpet artistry form the foundation of our featured selection.

**The Mughal Medallion — Heritage, Bhadohi**

Inspired by the enduring visual vocabulary of Mughal design, this collection celebrates symmetry, ornamental detailing and the richness of traditional Indian carpet aesthetics.

**The Jungle Reverie — Botanical, Mirzapur**

A meeting of nature and craftsmanship, drawing upon botanical expression and the organic language of the natural world.

**The Ivory Fold — Contemporary, Bhadohi**

An exploration of restraint, texture and contemporary elegance, conceived for spaces where subtlety makes its own statement.

**The Kashmir Indigo — Silk, Srinagar**

An expression of the depth and sophistication of indigo, complemented by the refined character of silk craftsmanship.

*Each collection represents a different design sensibility, united by an appreciation for material, detail and enduring artistry.*
